Iyazaki in Vancouver. Outstanding vibes even better with great company.

We started with a tofu with enoki mushrooms and green onions. Excellent contrast between the salty mushrooms and onions with a soft, bland tofu. Seems like it would be better if it were a little more balanced, without the mushrooms totally overtaking the dish.

![Tofu and enoki mushroom](/images/guu1/IMG_0395.jpeg)

Next we had essentially a hot dog with ketchup and sesame seeds, which was surprisingly good. A perfect hot dogn with the right amount of ketchup in each bite.

![Hot dog](/images/guu1/IMG_0396.jpeg)

Excellent green beans.

![Green Beans](/images/guu1/IMG_0397.jpeg)

And of course need some raw fish.

![Salmon and Tuna Tataki](/images/guu1/IMG_0398.jpeg)

Beef 1 was a flavorful flank steak, not pre chopped.

![Beef 1](/images/guu1/IMG_0400.jpeg)

Beef 2 was wagyu tataki with salmon roe, green onions over daikon. this was the best dish, a must get. Perfect balance between the very rare meat, saltiness of the salmon roe, and the crunchy daikon.

![Beef 2](/images/guu1/IMG_0402.jpeg)

Another interesting dish containing smoked salmon.

![Smoked Salmon](/images/guu1/IMG_0403.jpeg)

![Pickles](/images/guu1/IMG_0404.jpeg)

The only thing I didn't really like was the okra, although not because it tasted bad, but because of how slimy the okra was.

![Okra](/images/guu1/IMG_0405.jpeg)

Another star of the meal was the grilled octopus.

![Octopus](/images/guu1/IMG_0406.jpeg)

A surprising favorite was the dessert - cheesecake, whipped cream, and green tea ice cream. All were delicious, and definitely worth having.

Overall Guu obtains a score of 3/4 beans - will be returning if I return to vancouver.
